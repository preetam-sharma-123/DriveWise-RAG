## Engine and Performance
The Nexon is offered with a 1.2-litre 3-cylinder turbocharged petrol engine (available
in petrol and CNG form) producing up to 120 PS and 170 Nm of torque, and a 1.5-litre
4-cylinder diesel engine producing up to 115 PS and 260 Nm of torque. Transmission
choices include a 5-speed manual, 6-speed manual, 6-speed AMT, and 7-speed DCT on
petrol variants; a 6-speed manual or 6-speed AMT on diesel variants; and a 6-speed
manual on the CNG variant. All versions are front-wheel drive.

## Mileage and Fuel Efficiency
ARAI-certified mileage for the Nexon ranges from approximately 17 km/l up to 24.08
km/l depending on variant, engine, and transmission. The petrol variant is rated at
approximately 23.24 km/l, while the CNG variant returns approximately 17.44 km/kg.
Diesel variants sit toward the higher end of the range. Fuel tank capacity is
approximately 44 litres. Real-world mileage will be lower than ARAI figures and
depends on driving style, traffic, and load.

## Safety
The Nexon comes with six airbags as standard (driver, front passenger, two curtain,
and side airbags for driver and front passenger), ABS with EBD, electronic stability
program (ESP), traction control (ASR), and hill assist control. Other standard safety
features include ISOFIX child seat anchors, front and rear parking cameras, an
engine immobilizer, central locking, speed-sensing door lock, child safety lock, and
an over-speed warning system. The Nexon has received a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety
rating for both adult and child occupant protection. Higher trims add Level 1 ADAS
features such as autonomous emergency braking.

## Dimensions
The Nexon measures 3,995 mm in overall length, 1,804 mm in width, and 1,620 mm in
height, with a wheelbase of 2,498 mm. Ground clearance is approximately 208 mm. Fuel
tank capacity is approximately 44 litres.

## Interior and Comfort
The cabin includes ventilated front seats, a panoramic sunroof that can be operated
via voice command, cruise control, and a driver-focused instrument cluster.
Comfort-oriented features across trims include a rear middle three-point seatbelt,
adequate rear legroom for the segment, and multiple interior color/trim options
depending on the variant selected. Seating capacity is five.

## Infotainment and Connectivity
The Nexon offers a touchscreen infotainment system with wireless Android Auto and
Apple CarPlay support, along with Tata's connected-car technology suite for remote
vehicle monitoring and control. Premium sound system options are available on higher
trims. Steering-mounted controls are provided for audio and connectivity functions
across most variants.

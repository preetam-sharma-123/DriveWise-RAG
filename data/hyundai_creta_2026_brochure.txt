## Engine and Performance
The Creta is offered with two 1.5-litre engine options. The 1.5L MPi petrol engine (4
cylinders, 16 valves, DOHC) produces a maximum power of 84.4 kW (115 PS) at 6,300 rpm
and maximum torque of 143.8 Nm at 4,500 rpm. The 1.5L U2 CRDi diesel engine produces
85 kW (116 PS) at 4,000 rpm and 250 Nm of torque between 1,500-2,750 rpm. Transmission
options include a 6-speed manual (petrol and diesel), a 6-speed IVT automatic (petrol),
and a 6-speed automatic transmission (diesel). Drive mode select (Eco, Comfort, Sport)
and traction control modes (snow, sand, mud) are available on higher trims, along with
paddle shifters on automatic variants. Suspension is McPherson strut with coil spring at
the front and a coupled torsion beam axle at the rear, with disc brakes on all four wheels.

## Mileage and Fuel Efficiency
Based on ARAI certification, the 1.5L petrol manual variant returns approximately 17.4
km/l, while the petrol IVT automatic returns approximately 18.4 km/l. The 1.5L diesel
manual variant is the most fuel-efficient at approximately 21.8 km/l, with the diesel
automatic returning approximately 19.1 km/l. Fuel tank capacity is 50 litres across all
variants. Actual mileage will vary with driving conditions, load, and driving style.

## Safety
Six airbags (driver, passenger, side, and curtain) are standard across all variants,
along with ABS with EBD, electronic stability control (ESC), vehicle stability management
(VSM), and hill start assist control (HAC). Other standard safety features include rear
disc brakes, rear parking sensors, a tyre pressure monitoring system, speed alert system,
impact-sensing auto door unlock, speed-sensing auto door lock, front seatbelts with
pretensioners and height adjustment, driver and passenger seatbelt reminders, an
emergency stop signal (ESS), ISOFIX child seat anchors, and an immobilizer. Higher trims
add a rear camera with steering-adaptive parking guidelines, a driver rear-view monitor,
automatic headlamps, front fog lamps, a rear defogger with timer, and a burglar alarm.

## Dimensions
The Creta measures 4,300 mm in overall length, 1,790 mm in width, and 1,635 mm in
height (with roof rails). Wheelbase is 2,610 mm and fuel tank capacity is 50 litres.
Tyre sizes vary by variant: 205/65 R16 steel or styled wheels on base trims, and
215/60 R17 alloy wheels (clean silver, black, or diamond-cut depending on trim) on
higher trims. The spare wheel across all trims is a 205/65 R16 steel wheel.

## Interior and Comfort
Higher trims feature a voice-enabled smart panoramic sunroof, front-row ventilated
seats, an 8-way power driver seat, soothing blue ambient lighting, an electric parking
brake with auto hold, and an auto healthy air purifier. Comfort features across the
range include a 60:40 split rear seat, height-adjustable driver seat and front headrests,
front and rear power windows, a front console armrest with storage, and rear AC vents.
Top trims add a 2-step reclining rear seat, rear centre armrest with cup holders, rear
seat headrest cushions, and USB chargers front and rear. Interior themes include
two-tone black & greige, or all-black with contrast inserts on the Knight edition.

## Infotainment and Connectivity
Infotainment options range from a 20.32 cm (8.0") touchscreen audio system on
mid-range trims to a 26.03 cm (10.25") HD infotainment display with Bluelink on top
trims. Wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay are available, along with Bluetooth,
steering-mounted audio and voice-recognition controls, and an Arkamys sound mood
setting on select trims. Top trims include a Bose premium sound system with 8
speakers. The Hyundai Bluelink connected-car suite (with a 3-year free subscription)
offers over-the-air (OTA) map updates, remote engine start/stop, stolen vehicle
tracking and immobilization, a monthly health report, voice-operated sunroof control,
remote air purifier activation, a Bluelink-integrated smartwatch app, and Home-to-Car
control via Amazon Alexa and Google Voice Assistant. A smartphone wireless charger is
available from mid-range trims upward.
